Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a paid ChatGPT Plus subscription?

No! While we can build a Custom GPT if you have Plus, we can also host your custom bot as a standalone web URL that works on any browser without any paid subscriptions.

How fast do I get my bot?

Orders are typically delivered within 24 to 48 hours. If you have an imminent exam tomorrow, let us know in the notes and we will prioritize your build.

Is my data and course material private?

Yes. Your study materials and notes are used strictly to create your personal knowledge index and are never shared or made public.
